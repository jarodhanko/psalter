## Optional FluidR3_GM instrument packs

The optional nylon guitar and harpsichord samples in `public/soundfonts/optional-v1/`
are based on FluidR3_GM, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
United States. They were obtained from Paul Rosen's `midi-js-soundfonts` fork,
commit `cbd6b6f6d1af89ebfb69402860741288f08ff8b7`, on 2026-10-04. The fork credits
the original MIDI.js soundfont project (gleitz / MIDI.js).

- Source and credits: https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts/blob/cbd6b6f6d1af89ebfb69402860741288f08ff8b7/README.md
- Original samples: https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/cbd6b6f6d1af89ebfb69402860741288f08ff8b7/FluidR3_GM
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Changes: 176 original MP3 files are copied unchanged. Each instrument adds a
`Db8.mp3` derived from its own `C8.mp3` using FFmpeg 7.1.5
(`asetrate=46722,aresample=44100`, `libmp3lame -q:a 2`). Both derived files retain
the same CC BY 3.0 US license. Detailed source blob hashes, derivation filter,
final bytes, and aggregate SHA-256 digests are recorded in
`docs/audio/optional-instruments-provenance.json` and the adjacent upstream
manifests. Runtime per-sample SHA-256 checks use
`src/features/viewer/optionalInstrumentManifest.json`.

These two packs download only when selected. They are excluded from the app's
initial offline precache; a successful complete download is saved for offline
playback when browser storage permits. This does not change the three existing
fully precached instruments.

Audio source-only distribution and reproduction

This source repository excludes all MP3 binaries. The explicit audio:prepare setup fetches the pinned upstream originals and creates the three documented Db8 extensions. See docs/audio/setup.md and docs/audio/source-manifest.json for exact pins and reproduction requirements. A prepared distribution retains every notice above. The organ Db8 can be reproduced with FFmpeg 7.1.5 plus hash-gated historical encoder-label/length/CRC metadata reconstruction; it has identical audio frames and decoded PCM and retains the historical MP3 bytes. This reconstruction does not mean FFmpeg 4.4 was run. Guitar and harpsichord reproduce directly with the documented FFmpeg 7.1.5 recipe.
