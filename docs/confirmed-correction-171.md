This bounded change adds one independently corroborated music correction to
source the historical reviewed source. It changes only the bass
at quarter onset 35 in WEBB, “God in Nature,” Psalm 65, George J. Webb, B-flat
major and 4/4. F3 eighth plus E-flat3 eighth becomes the printed F3 quarter.

## Independent source proof

RFPA ebook PDF page 254 contains the complete score; the original 1912 scan
spans leaves 0152–0153, printed pages 144–145. Both source identities, URLs and
hashes are recorded in the correction-evidence fixture. Both prints show the
filled lower F3 head on the second bass-staff line with its own unflagged
downstem, separate from the upper G3 upstem. There is no second lower E-flat3
head or eighth beam. The coordinator independently corroborated the reading
before the isolated source patch was prepared.

The source-owned fixture reconstructs all 223 written events, including 211
attacks, twelve quarter rests and both slurs, directly from the revised manual
ledger and separately reconciles it to the original runtime oracle. All three
soprano lyric rows are preserved. The inverse source span must restore the
previous ABC exactly, and must fail both printed-music and audio comparisons.
The tests also reject the disputed q44 B-flat2 reading, removal of either slur,
an unsupported half-note pickup, loss of the genuine q40/q40.5 bass eighths,
and a change of the word “gentle.”

The existing three exact W: prose witnesses remain active and unchanged. No
whole-ABC guard or legacy print-selection digest applies to this arrangement,
so no guard is added or rebound. All 75 existing legacy witnesses remain
covered by the complete loaded-source registry regression.

## Preserved qualifications and rejected readings

The initial q44 bass B-flat2 proposal was rejected: both prints and canonical
source agree on C3. The nine initially unflattened alto scientific-pitch names
omitted the stated E-flat key signature; these ledger errors remain in the
retained rejection history and do not become score edits.

The original opening tenor head appears hollow on the shared quarter stem;
RFPA fills it. The RFPA quarter timing stays unchanged. The older head's
appearance remains a source qualification, not permission to alter duration.

RFPA's compact “gentle” at soprano q51/q52 has an ambiguous syllable boundary.
The initial ledger inferred “gen-tle”; canonical w: retains “gent-le” and W:
retains “gen-tle,” both reconstructing “gentle.” The fixture and tests preserve
this named uncertainty, and a changed word must fail. Shared interstaff text
does not independently establish all lower-only pronunciation onsets. Exact
punctuation is not certified by the lexical test; the original strict audit
retains its straight/curly apostrophe differences. Literal w:/W: lines must
remain byte-identical to the base.

At q15, q31 and q63, alto/bass quarter rests remain invisible when selected
without their upper partner. This known selected-part presentation issue is
explicitly retained. Printed half notes must remain literal half notes; equal
sounding durations do not waive a notation mismatch.
