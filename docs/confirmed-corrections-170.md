## Confirmed music

Psalter 170, MENDEBRAS, is in F major and 4/4. The complete matching RFPA
engraving is on PDF page 253. The independent original 1912 scan is leaf 0152,
printed page 144. The source evidence fixture identifies both URLs and hashes.

- Soprano segment 1 final F4/G4 pair at quarter onsets 4 and 4.5: straight
  eighths replace dotted eighth plus sixteenth.
- Soprano segment 7 final F4/G4 pair at quarter onsets 20 and 20.5: the same
  straight-eighth correction in the repeated opening music.
- Tenor segment 20 at quarter onset 54: F3 half replaces C4 half. Both prints
  show the hollow F3 head on the second bass-staff line, shared with the bass
  through separate stems. There is no C4 ledger-line head.

The initial segment 1 rhythm uncertainty was independently resolved by the
native-pixel comparison to the true dotted pickup: the F4 dot and separate
second beam are absent. Its original uncertainty, partial patch, negative
control and subsequent coordinator corroboration remain in retained evidence.
The genuine dotted pickups at q0 and q16 stay unchanged. The original six-bar
repeat and RFPA expanded version have the same performed structure.

partner is absent. The source contains those rests. This known selected-part
presentation issue remains explicit and is not repaired by this music patch.

permits only the two named syllable qualifications; the literal canonical lyric
