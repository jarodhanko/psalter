# Corpus provenance and rights notes

## Musical source

The core ABC corpus is a page-by-page transcription of *The Psalter with Responsive Readings* (United Presbyterian Board of Publication and Sabbath-School Work, 1912). Later photographed addenda, including Psalters 433–450 as available, are recorded separately and are not presumed part of the 1912 public-domain edition. The working sources are user-supplied annotated or photographed pages; those images are verification evidence and are not part of the distributable application. Catalog metadata for the core edition is available from [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/psalterwithrespo00unit) and [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL25489678M/The_Psalter_with_responsive_readings).

The project rule is strict: the photograph for each setting controls notes, lyrics, tune boundaries, and alternate settings. Other hymnals, public indexes, MIDI files, and MusicXML files may help locate or check a setting, but they never override the photographed page merely because they are easier to parse.

Psalm-number search metadata was cross-checked against an independent 1912 catalog. Psalter numbers 433 and 434 are Lord's Prayer settings rather than Psalm settings.

## Rights review

The base edition was published in the United States in 1912. The U.S. Copyright Office's current duration guidance states that works published in the United States before January 1, 1931 are in the public domain in the United States. See [Copyright Office Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf). This conclusion is jurisdiction-specific and does not establish the status of the work outside the United States.

That date does not by itself settle every individual setting or later contribution reproduced in the working corpus. The corpus has 88 settings flagged for printed copyright, permission, or ownership notices: 53 from the photographed 1912 U.S. edition (41 dated and twelve undated), two newly retained undated source-image notices (nos. 78 and 92), two later 1947 settings, and thirty-one photo-verified 1934 settings included in the shared viewer. The individual historical notice inventory and qualifications are maintained in [rights-audit.md](rights-audit.md).

Numbers 433 and 434 are later Lord's Prayer settings registered in 1947, not Psalm settings. A bounded search found no matching renewal in the four official 1974/75 Music Catalog of Copyright Entries volumes. That is supportive negative evidence with search limitations, not certified clearance. Together with the later revision-committee statement, the review has not established a current-rights basis requiring their removal. See the newer assessment in [licensing.md](licensing.md); preserve all notice metadata and territory qualifications.

Later editions, arrangements, recordings, artwork, and other separately authored material can have distinct rights even when an underlying work is public domain; see [Copyright Office Circular 22](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ22.pdf). This repository's transcription must be checked against the photographed source rather than copied from a later edition or arrangement.

## Corrections

Every correction should record:

- the Psalter number and tune/variant;
- the photographed page(s) checked;
- the exact musical or textual difference;
- whether the correction changes notes, rhythm, voices, lyrics, or metadata;
- the full validation results after the change.

Do not commit source photographs, private annotations, or scratch conversion artifacts to the public repository unless their distribution rights and privacy have been reviewed separately.
