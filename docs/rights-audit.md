# Corpus rights audit (U.S. only)

## Current assessment

This historical notice inventory is retained for provenance and validator coverage. The newer [licensing assessment](licensing.md#psalter-current-evidence-not-presumptive-removal) supersedes older presumptive-blocker wording: no removal requirement has been established for nos. 1–434. The exact 1954 harmony of no. 442 remains unresolved after a bounded renewal search and an unsuccessful search for the exact supplement page. No absolute or worldwide clearance is claimed.

## Scope and caution

This is a provenance and release-triage record, not legal advice or a legal opinion. It addresses only U.S. copyright questions indicated by the photographed source and the research recorded below. It does not determine rights outside the United States, nor does it clear later editions, new arrangements, recordings, artwork, photographs, typography, or other separately authored material.

The core corpus is transcribed from the photographed 1912 U.S. edition of *The Psalter with Responsive Readings*; later photographed addenda are identified separately below. Edition metadata is available from [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/psalterwithrespo00unit) and [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL25489678M/The_Psalter_with_responsive_readings). The source photographs are verification evidence and are not included in the distributable application.

## Base-edition conclusion

The U.S. Copyright Office states in [Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) that works published in the United States before January 1, 1931 are in the public domain in the United States. On that basis, the 1912 base edition is treated as public domain **in the United States**. That conclusion does not automatically resolve separately protected contributions printed in, or later added to, the corpus.

[Circular 22](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ22.pdf) explains that later editions and arrangements may carry separate copyright interests. Therefore, release review must distinguish the underlying 1912 source from any later edition, arrangement, recording, visual art, or source used to transcribe or verify it.

Nos. 414–432 are in the numbered core sequence but their photographed settings explicitly read “Words and music copyrighted by the Publication Committee of the Christian Reformed Church, 1934” and credit 1931 text versions. They cannot inherit the 1912 edition's U.S. public-domain conclusion merely because their numbers are below 435. The bounded follow-up research has not established a removal requirement; it does not certify every component or territory.

## 1954 harmony credit in Psalter 442: unresolved

The photographed p. 402 credits Henry Bruinsma with a 1954 harmonization of ZION'S GATES and William Kuipers with a 1931 text. No separate copyright-notice sentence is visible on that page, so no. 442 is not included in the `N:`/`W:` printed-notice count above. [Hymnary's Blue Psalter entry](https://hymnary.org/hymn/BPsH/165) identifies both text and tune/harmony as copyrighted by the Christian Reformed Church Publication Committee. This is a triage lead, not a substitute for a rights determination or the user's photographed notes. Preserve attribution and treat the later harmony/text as unresolved for public distribution.

## 1947 Heys settings: bounded negative renewal evidence

Psalter nos. 433 and 434 are Heys Lord's Prayer settings with exact 1947 registrations **EP17230** and **EP17231**, respectively. The initial copyright term ran to September 5, 1975. A search of all four official 1974/75 Music Catalog of Copyright Entries volumes located no matching renewal entry:

- [1974 Music CCE, volume 1](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig32852lib)
- [1974 Music CCE, volume 2](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig32852libr)
- [1975 Music CCE, volume 1](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig32952library)
- [1975 Music CCE, volume 2](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig32952libr)

This is useful negative research, not proof that no renewal occurred. OCR, indexing, name variants, and record-search limitations can produce false negatives, and no rights successor has been established. No current-rights basis requiring their removal has been established. Do not treat this bounded search alone as certified clearance; see the corroborating evidence and limits in licensing.md.
