The owner's original annotated photos 014–015 were not available for this fresh review; the historical photo-QA comment describes the earlier notation/first-stanza review.

ANVERN's repeated closing phrase and its melisma placeholders are omitted because RIVAULX has no corresponding repeated phrase. The existing first-stanza row and every SATB music/header field remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No catalog inheritance or general renderer behavior is changed.
