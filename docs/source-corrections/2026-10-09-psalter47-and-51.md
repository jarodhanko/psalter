# Psalter47 and Psalter51 printed pitch corrections

Fourteen note pitches are corrected from independently corroborated print readings; no lyric text, note duration, onset, barline, tie, slur, voice assignment, or accidental is changed.

Sources: The Psalter (1912), pp. 45 and 49, Internet Archive item `psalterwithrespo00unit`, leaves 0051 and 0055; RFPA matching edition PDF pages 84–85 and 92–93. Actual original scan pixels corroborate both arrangements. The #47 ledger was unblinded; #51 was entered before opening canonical ABC. This is not a claim of owner-photo verification.

## Underlay and evidence limits

#47 stanza 1 is engraved in both editions, stanzas 2–5 in RFPA only, and stanzas 6–11 are prose in both. Placement of those six prose stanzas remains editorial scansion. #51 stanza 1 is engraved in both editions and stanzas 2–3 in RFPA; the app retains its `rev'-rence` apostrophe placement against the printed `rev-’rence`.

The regression fixture copies independently read pitch/duration and lyric ledgers. It exercises all 15 nonempty voice selections, transpositions 0/−2/+2, native stanza splitting, learner accidentals, real ScoreRenderer preparation and afterParsing for screen/print lyric and music-only copies, and the real playback callback/muting path. It also checks exactly seven changed sounding pitches per song with all other sounding fields stable.

SVG drawing is stubbed in component tests. These checks establish prepared models and callback behavior, not browser geometry or acoustic listening. Focused validation: TypeScript `tsc --noEmit` passed. 177 tests passed across the new 108-test regression suite, both Drostane integration suites (68 tests), and the Psalter50 courtesy-flat test. The no-generation Vitest config preserves the normal React/jsdom/VM-thread settings; its omitted PWA plugin caused a dependency-scan warning for the unrelated virtual PWA import. This historical focused result is not a current build, browser, full-suite, or deployment verification.
