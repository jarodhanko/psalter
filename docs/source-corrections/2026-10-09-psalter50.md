# Psalter 50: printed Alto courtesy flat

Both original 1912 printed page 48 (Internet Archive `psalterwithrespo00unit`, JP2 leaf0054) and the later RFPA engraving (PDF pages90–91) print an explicit flat on Alto E4 at q44, event38, under “re-” in “rely” in the third system. The independent auditor and integrator inspected both source images.

The canonical note already sounds E-flat through its key signature. Adding `_` to this one `E2` preserves the printed courtesy sign without changing pitch, duration, words, or bar structure. The surrounding phrase becomes `D2 G2 F2 _E2 | D6`.

The source audit compared all231 vocal events. The candidate retained540 prepared-display/underlay cases,45 actual audio-callback cases and15 music-only selections. Source guard inventory remained inactive. Actual ABCJS SVG inspection confirmed the added note-owned flat in ordinary full and Alto-only views, while learner mode retained its existing sign. That inspection used synthetic geometry and does not establish native visual acceptance.

The regression test checks the note-owned accidental at the independently identified q44, its quarter duration, and whole-score MIDI equivalence to the previous implicit spelling. Source pixels, hashes, ledgers and full audit qualifications are retained in the per-number audit evidence.
