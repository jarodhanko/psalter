About Old Psalter

Old Psalter is an online Psalter viewer for reading and singing from sheet music. Search selections, transpose a score into a comfortable key, hear individual parts, and prepare printable service booklets.

The core collection comes from The Psalter (1912), with later addenda recorded separately. Two additional hymnbooks, Traditional Hymns and Traditional and Contemporary Hymns, are available from the Library.

Find and practice a selection

  1. Open the viewer and choose a Psalter number, or search by title, tune, composer, lyrics, or encoded Psalm reference. Use the Library to change books.
  2. Open Score settings to change the key, score size, tempo, instrument, or displayed parts. The available parts follow the selected edition.
  3. Press Play to hear the selected parts. Stop playback before changing practice settings, then play again. For Psalter selections with a Psalm reference, you can also open the corresponding King James Version text.

Save, share, and print

Save favorites and named song lists in the Library. Print an individual selection or prepare a service booklet with saved keys, parts, and verses. Share links preserve the selected song and viewer settings, not your private saved library.

Favorites, recent selections, and lists stay in this browser. They do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data removes them. The installable app can keep notation and downloaded instruments available offline when browser storage permits.

Editable notation and continuing review

The app includes the notation source, not just pictures of score pages. Download current ABC exports portable notation reflecting the displayed parts, key, verse grouping or music-only setting, and tempo, with source credits and rights notes. These text files can be edited in compatible ABC tools; Old Psalter is not an in-app score editor. Exporting does not alter the underlying source, and some visual or playback details can differ in other ABC tools.

Maintaining the collection involves repeated comparisons with historical source pages and checks of notation, score layout, and playback. Review and corrections continue: this process is not a guarantee that every setting or device is error-free. Reports from singers and readers help identify what needs another check.

Sources and edition notes

The historical score is the transcription authority. The core source is The Psalter with Responsive Readings (1912). Photographed later addenda and the additional hymn editions are distinct sources, not all part of that publication.

Source credits, notices, and edition qualifications remain with the music. Later arrangements, texts, and recordings can have different rights and territory restrictions; the age of the core Psalter does not establish worldwide clearance for every item. See the selected edition's notes and the third-party notices for the included software and audio assets.

Help improve a score

If reporting is enabled on this site and you find a notation, lyric, playback, or layout problem, open the affected selection and choose Report an issue below the score. Describe what differs and where it occurs. The report includes the selected score settings; please do not include names, contact details, or other personal information.

AI-assisted reports are welcome when they describe a reproducible problem and include the selection, settings, expected result, and supporting source. Please review them before submitting and avoid duplicate, speculative, or bulk submissions.
